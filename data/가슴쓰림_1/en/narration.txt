Waking up at 3am with a sour, burning taste in your throat? Nearly a third of Americans get heartburn weekly, most of it at night.

Lying flat removes the gravity that keeps acid down, and coffee, spicy food, and tomato sauce make it worse.

Coffee relaxes the muscle holding acid down - have it with food, and cut it off a few hours before bed.

Spicy food irritates the esophagus and slows digestion. Cut hot sauce to a small drizzle, or season with basil and oregano instead.

Tomato sauce is naturally acidic - a pinch of baking soda stirred in neutralizes a good chunk of it.

None of these need to disappear completely - just watch the timing, and prop your head up if reflux wakes you at night.
