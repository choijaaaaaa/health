Still exhausted, foggy, and stuck at the same weight even though you take your thyroid pill every single morning? It might not be your dose that's the problem.

Levothyroxine needs an empty stomach and a clear runway to actually get absorbed into your bloodstream. A handful of everyday foods and drinks can bind to it or block it before your body ever gets the full dose - coffee, soy, and walnuts are the three biggest offenders.

Coffee is one of the most common ones. Even a small cup taken too close to your pill can cut how much of your dose actually gets absorbed by up to 30%, according to research on the interaction.

Try waiting at least 30 to 60 minutes after your pill before your first sip - that gap alone lets your body absorb what you actually swallowed.

Soy is the sneaky one. Soy milk, tofu, and other soy products bind to levothyroxine in your gut and block a big chunk of it from ever reaching your bloodstream.

Keep soy at least 4 hours away from your dose - a soy latte right after your pill undoes a lot of what the medication is supposed to do.

Walnuts work the same way. The fiber and compounds in them bind to the medication and drag your absorption down, whether they're a snack or mixed into breakfast.

Same rule applies - give it 4 hours between your pill and a handful of walnuts.

None of this means giving up coffee, soy, or walnuts for good - it's really just about timing. Take your pill on an empty stomach first thing, then give it that window before anything else touches your system.
