Turns out, a few everyday foods could be why your hands and feet feel numb, cold, or swollen by the end of the day.

Deli meat is loaded with sodium - a two-ounce serving of turkey packs about 570 milligrams, over a third of your daily limit in one sandwich. Swap in freshly roasted chicken breast instead.

Fried food raises LDL cholesterol and narrows your arteries over time. Air-frying or baking at home cuts most of that oil out.

And sugary soda alone is linked to a nearly 20 percent higher heart disease risk. Swap it for sparkling water with real fruit juice.

Small swaps like these add up to real relief for your circulation.
