About 1 in 2 women over 50 will break a bone from bone loss they never even felt coming - here's what's speeding it up.

Soda is the biggest one. Phosphoric acid in cola pulls calcium out of your body - one study found regular cola drinkers had close to 4% lower hip bone density. Swap it for sparkling water or a calcium-fortified drink.

Coffee flushes calcium out through urine, especially once you're past 4 cups a day. Cap it around 2 to 3 cups, with a splash of milk.

And heavy drinking - more than 2 drinks a day - is linked to a 38% higher fracture risk, partly because it blocks your body from activating vitamin D. Build in a few alcohol-free days and get some daily sunlight.

Watch what you're drinking, and pair it with enough calcium to protect your bones.
