You knock back a coffee the second you open your eyes, then feel shaky and starving by mid-morning — that's not just you.

Cortisol starts high the moment you wake up and keeps climbing for the next 30 to 45 minutes, and caffeine hitting an empty stomach piles right on top of that rise. One University of Guelph study found drinking coffee before breakfast raised the blood sugar response to that same meal by about 50% compared to coffee after eating.

Here's what actually helps: eggs, cottage cheese, or a later cup of coffee.

A couple of scrambled eggs before your coffee gives your body protein first, which blunts that glucose swing.

No time to cook? A bowl of cottage cheese with berries does the same job in under a minute.

Or the simplest fix — push your coffee back to 45 minutes to an hour after waking, once your cortisol has already peaked and started coming back down.

You don't have to quit coffee. Just don't make it the very first thing your stomach sees.
