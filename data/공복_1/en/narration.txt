Still not losing fat even though you're eating less? It's about what hits your stomach first.

Starting your day with refined carbs spikes your blood sugar and insulin — and high insulin tells your body to store fat, not burn it.

Try these on an empty stomach: eggs, chia seed water, apple cider vinegar, and green tea.

Eggs are seriously filling — one study found people who had eggs instead of a bagel ate less for the next 36 hours.

Chia seeds form a gel in your stomach — a tablespoon in water curbed hunger before lunch in one trial.

Diluted apple cider vinegar is linked to lower body weight and waist size across a meta-analysis of ten clinical trials.

Green tea's catechins may support fat burning — one trial linked it to more abdominal fat loss when paired with exercise.

You don't need all four at once — just start with whichever feels easiest.
