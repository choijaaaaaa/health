Grilled burgers and a few beers last night — and your big toe's throbbing so bad you can't get a sock on this morning? That's gout, caused by sharp uric acid crystals building up in the joint.

Beer alone can raise uric acid over 6%, and just two beers can double your flare risk within a day.

Red meat is loaded with purines too, so the more you eat, the more uric acid builds up in your joints.

Sugary soda isn't safe either — two or more a day is linked to an 85% higher gout risk.

Swap beer for water, and snack on cherries instead — a study linked eating cherries to a 35% lower risk of gout attacks. Trade red meat for tofu, and swap soda for unsweetened tea.
