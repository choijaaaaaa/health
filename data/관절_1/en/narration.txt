Knees ache on the stairs, mornings stiff until you move - a few everyday foods could be feeding that joint inflammation.

Soda, fried food, and deli meat are the big three. Women who drink one soda a day have a 63% higher risk of rheumatoid arthritis, found in a Harvard study of 80,000 women.

Fried food throws off your omega-6 to omega-3 balance, and processed meat raises CRP, your body's inflammation marker - one study found 50 extra grams a day raised it over 38%.

Swap soda for tart cherry juice, shown to lower CRP. Cut back on fried food and add a daily handful of walnuts for anti-inflammatory omega-3s. And swap deli meat for tofu or beans a few times a week - plant protein is linked to lower CRP too.
