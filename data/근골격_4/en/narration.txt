Fell asleep scrolling on your phone and woke up unable to turn your head? A few daily habits might be feeding that.

Looking down at a phone or laptop for hours keeps neck and shoulder muscles tense and slows blood flow. Low magnesium makes it harder for those muscles to relax, and coffee, salty snacks, and alcohol can make it worse.

Drinking more than two cups of coffee a day has been linked to more frequent neck and shoulder pain, possibly because caffeine keeps muscles braced and mildly dehydrated. Cutting back to one cup and drinking extra water may help ease that.

Fast food and salty snacks can crowd out magnesium-rich whole foods, leaving you short on the mineral your muscles need to relax.

Alcohol drains magnesium and disrupts the deep sleep your muscles need to recover overnight.

Cut back on coffee, salty snacks, and alcohol, and add more whole foods like chickpeas and salmon to your meals for their magnesium and omega-3s.
