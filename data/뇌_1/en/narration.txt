Walking into a room and blanking on why you're there? A new Tufts University study points to three everyday foods behind it: ultra-processed snacks, processed meat, and sugary drinks.

That study tracked over 10,000 adults for eight years - people over 50 getting a fifth of their calories from ultra-processed snacks and frozen meals showed cognitive decline about 35% faster. Swap the chip bag for nuts or berries.

Processed meat matters too - a 2025 study found eating it daily, even just two strips of bacon, raised dementia risk 13%, adding roughly 1.6 years of cognitive aging per additional daily serving. Try swapping in nuts, legumes, chicken, or fish instead - nuts and legumes alone were linked to a 20% lower risk.

Sugary drinks hit memory hardest - Boston University's Framingham Heart Study found drinking more than two a day matched memory scores 11 years older, plus a smaller hippocampus. Reach for sparkling water instead - same fizz, none of the sugar.
