Third energy drink of the day at your desk, and now your eyelid won't stop twitching through the meeting? Stacking 300mg or more of caffeine a day is one of the biggest triggers, and it's usually a few things in your cup.

The nerve running your eyelid gets overexcitable, and low magnesium makes that worse - coffee, energy drinks, and alcohol are the big three.

Coffee's caffeine keeps that nerve switched on and flushes out magnesium at the same time - stack an energy drink on top and you've likely blown past 300mg before lunch.

Alcohol relaxes you at first, but the dehydration and magnesium loss bring the twitch back worse the next day.

Switch to decaf, swap the energy drink for sparkling water or an electrolyte drink, keep alcohol to one or two, and refill magnesium with nuts or leafy greens.
