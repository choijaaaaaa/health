If you've started a GLP-1 weight loss shot like Ozempic or Wegovy, and one eye suddenly gets blurry or part of your vision goes dark, don't brush it off.

That combination can point to NAION, a condition where blood flow to the optic nerve gets cut off. Doctors sometimes call it a "stroke of the eye."

A 2024 Harvard Medical School study found the risk climbs for a few reasons. First, patients with diabetes on semaglutide were over four times more likely to be diagnosed with NAION than diabetics who weren't on the drug. Second, people using it purely for weight loss, without diabetes, showed an even steeper jump - more than seven times the risk.

Researchers haven't proven the drug directly causes this - the study was small, and other health conditions can play a role too. Still, eye specialists recommend a baseline eye exam before starting, especially if you're over 55 or already have vascular risk factors.

If vision changes hit while you're on the medication, treat it as urgent and get to an eye doctor the same day. Vision loss from NAION can become permanent, so acting early matters most.
