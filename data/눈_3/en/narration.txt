Dark circles showing up right on schedule every spring or fall, alongside the sneezing and congestion? Over 80 million Americans deal with seasonal allergies, and the same pollen that clogs your sinuses can pool blood under your thin under-eye skin too - caffeine, alcohol, and salt each make it worse.

Coffee's caffeine acts as a diuretic, pulling water from that thin skin and making the vessels underneath stand out more.

Alcohol dehydrates you too, and dilates those same vessels, so they look darker and more pooled the next morning.

Salty snacks like chips and fries make your body hold onto extra water, and that puffiness often shows up first under your eyes.

Add water alongside your coffee, keep alcohol to one or two drinks with water between, and swap chips or fries for unsalted nuts or fresh fruit instead.
