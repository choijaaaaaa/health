Those dark circles under your eyes - sleep isn't always the reason. A few everyday drinks and snacks could be feeding them too.

Skin under your eyes is some of the thinnest on your body, so blood vessels underneath show right through. Caffeine, alcohol, and salt each make that worse.

Coffee's caffeine acts as a diuretic, pulling water from that thin skin and making the vessels stand out more.

Alcohol dehydrates you too, and dilates those same vessels, so they look darker and more pooled the next morning.

Salty snacks like chips and fries make your body hold onto extra water, and that puffiness often shows up first under your eyes.

Switch to decaf or add water alongside your coffee, keep alcohol to one or two drinks with water between, and swap chips or fries for unsalted nuts or fresh fruit instead.
