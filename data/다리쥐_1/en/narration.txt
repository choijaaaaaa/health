Nearly 1 in 3 adults over 50 get jolted awake by a leg cramp at night, and it's not always about age.

Low magnesium, potassium, and calcium make muscles cramp more easily. Coffee, alcohol, and salty processed foods are the big three that drain them fast.

Caffeine flushes out calcium and magnesium through urine. Alcohol does the same, but faster - especially right before bed.

Salty processed foods pile on sodium and throw off that balance, raising your cramp risk.

Cut off coffee earlier in the day, match alcohol with water, and swap chips for nuts or deli meat for chicken and tofu.
