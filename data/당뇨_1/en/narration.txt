Did you know 1 in 3 American adults already has prediabetes, and most don't know it? Watch for one sign, and two risky habits: skin changes, sitting too much, and skimping on sleep.

Dark, velvety patches on your neck or armpits that won't wash off - that's acanthosis nigricans, a sign of insulin resistance. Get it checked by a doctor.

Sitting eight-plus hours without a break lets insulin resistance build up quietly. Standing or walking two minutes every half hour can cut after-meal blood sugar by a third.

Sleeping less than six hours raises cortisol and blunts insulin sensitivity - linked to roughly a 30% higher diabetes risk. Protecting seven-plus hours most nights helps insulin do its job again.
