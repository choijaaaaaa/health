Still exhausted and stuck at the same weight even though you take your thyroid pill every morning? It might not be your dose that's the problem.

Coffee can cut how much levothyroxine your body actually absorbs by up to 30% if you drink it too soon. Wait 30 to 60 minutes after your pill before that first sip.

Soy binds to the medication in your gut and blocks a big chunk of it from reaching your bloodstream. Keep soy milk or tofu at least 4 hours from your dose.

Walnuts work the same way - give it that same 4-hour gap.

It's not about giving these up for good, just timing. Take your pill on an empty stomach and give it room to work before anything else touches your system.
