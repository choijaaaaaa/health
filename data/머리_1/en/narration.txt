Been through two or three anti-dandruff shampoos and the flakes still keep coming back? The bottle in your shower might not be the problem - your plate could be working against it.

A yeast called Malassezia lives on your scalp's oil, and breaking it down releases oleic acid that irritates skin in people sensitive to it. Three foods in the American diet can each fuel that cycle.

First, sweets like donuts and cookies spike blood sugar fast, raising insulin and IGF-1, which tells oil glands to make more sebum for the yeast.

Second, dairy like whole milk and ice cream. One clinical study found dairy among the foods most linked to flare-ups, and researchers point to hormones in milk that push oil glands into overdrive.

Third, fried food like French fries and fried chicken. Frying oils are heavy in omega-6 fats, which may drive inflammation and shift your sebum's makeup.

Try berries or nuts instead of donuts and cookies, unsweetened soy milk instead of whole milk, and oven-roasted or grilled versions instead of fried. Salmon and other fatty fish can help too.
