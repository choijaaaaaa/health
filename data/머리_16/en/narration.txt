Noticed more hair coming out in the shower since starting Ozempic, Wegovy, or Mounjaro?

Clinical trials confirm this — hair loss showed up in about 3% of Wegovy users versus 1% on placebo, and up to 5.7% on Mounjaro versus under 1% on placebo. Three things drive it: rapid weight loss itself, nutrient gaps from eating less, and nausea that blocks absorption.

First, losing weight quickly shocks your system. Your body redirects energy away from non-essential functions like hair growth, and follicles that were actively growing shift into a resting phase all at once — that's the sudden shedding.

Second, a smaller appetite means less protein, iron, and zinc reaching your follicles. Without those building blocks, hair grows in thinner and falls out faster.

Third, the nausea common with these medications means even the food you do eat doesn't get fully absorbed, worsening the nutrient gap.

Slow your rate of weight loss and prioritize protein, iron, and zinc-rich foods to help follicles recover. Growth typically resumes once weight stabilizes — talk to your doctor about adjusting your dose rather than stopping on your own.
