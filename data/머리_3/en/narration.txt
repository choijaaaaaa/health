80 million Americans have thinning hair, and genetics isn't the whole story.

Sudden shifts in diet and supplements can push follicles into shedding mode faster - sugary drinks, crash diets, and vitamin megadoses are the three to watch.

Soda and energy drinks spike blood sugar fast, and that's linked to inflammation that wears down hair follicles.

Strict keto or long fasting can shock your body into thinking food is scarce, cutting hair growth first.

Even hair vitamins can backfire - too much vitamin A is toxic and can trigger the same shedding it's meant to stop.

Swap soda for water, ease into diets instead of slashing calories overnight, and check your supplement label instead of stacking more.
