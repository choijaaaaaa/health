Takeout for dinner, a glass of wine, and a diet soda after - your temples throbbing by morning.

Migraine triggers work differently, and red wine, MSG-heavy takeout, and diet soda are common everyday culprits.

Red wine has far more histamine than white wine, and the surge can trigger a migraine within hours - 11% of sufferers call it their top trigger.

MSG in takeout and packaged snacks is a recognized trigger - regular intake caused headaches in healthy adults within five days.

Aspartame in diet soda is reported as a trigger nearly three times more often in migraine sufferers than in others.

Cutting out a suspected trigger for a few weeks, then adding it back alone, is how doctors often help patients pin down their real trigger.
