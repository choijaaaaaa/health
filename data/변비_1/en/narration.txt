Reaching for a bag of chips at your desk again? That everyday habit might be part of why you're still backed up.

About 1 in 5 US adults deals with chronic constipation, and diet is one of the biggest everyday factors behind it.

Chips and other processed snacks are stripped of fiber and loaded with sodium. Cheese's casein digests slowly. And skipping water makes stool even harder to pass.

Swap chips for popcorn or roasted chickpeas, ease up on dairy, and try 2 to 3 kiwis a day - it can work as well as fiber supplements.
