Up for the third time before 2 a.m. just to pee, and it burns worse every time you go? Something in your daily diet could be feeding that irritation.

Urologists point out that the bladder wall reacts to whatever ends up concentrated in your urine - some everyday drinks and foods make that reaction, and the burning that comes with it, noticeably worse.

Caffeine is the trigger doctors flag most often. Coffee and tea directly stimulate the bladder and increase how much urine you produce, so you're back in the bathroom sooner.

Diet soda's artificial sweeteners can aggravate an already-inflamed bladder lining, making burning and urgency worse with every can.

Orange juice and other citrus fruit are naturally high in citric acid, and that acidity can directly irritate an already-inflamed bladder lining as it passes through.

None of these need to disappear for good. Swap coffee for water or herbal tea, diet soda for plain still water, and citrus for a lower-acid fruit like banana. Drinking plenty of plain water throughout the day also helps dilute urine and ease the irritation.
