Bloated by mid-afternoon even though you barely ate? If you're deep into a high-protein routine, three parts of it might be why.

Added fibers and fast protein doses ferment or back up your gut, and it shows up constantly in "fitness" foods.

High-fiber protein bars often get that fiber from chicory root, mostly inulin your gut can't break down - bacteria ferment it in the colon, and about 10 grams is enough to trigger gas.

Check labels for chicory root, and build fiber from whole foods like berries or oats instead.

Whey shakes carry real milk lactose, and a big post-workout dose is a lot to process at once - undigested lactose ferments fast.

Switch to whey isolate, which strips out most of that lactose, or space shakes out instead of doubling up.

Cutting carbs hard for keto drops fiber fast too, since whole grains and fruit are where most people get theirs - less fiber means slower digestion, and that backup is its own bloating.

Keep chia seeds, avocado, and leafy greens in the rotation to stay regular.
