Notice extra itching and burning down there the morning after wing night with hot sauce and a few beers? What you're eating and drinking regularly could be feeding those hemorrhoid flare-ups.

Those veins are already under pressure from straining on the toilet - and certain foods pile on more pressure or irritate the area directly, which is why flare-ups often follow a heavy night.

Hot peppers and spicy sauces contain capsaicin, which can irritate the anal lining directly, making bowel movements burn and sting more.

Beer and wine dehydrate your body, hardening stool, and also widen blood vessels - both can make bleeding and pain worse.

Processed meats and other low-fiber foods tend to cause constipation, and straining harder to pass a hard stool adds more pressure to those veins, which can worsen symptoms.

Try swapping hot peppers for milder seasoning, alternating alcohol with water and cutting back on the amount, and replacing processed meat with vegetables or whole grains. Drinking enough water can help too.
