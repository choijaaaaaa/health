## 반전 각도
손톱이 마르면 갈라지니 물을 머금으면 나아질 것 같지만, 젖었다 마르기를 되풀이할수록 세포층 사이가 벌어져 갈라진다.
나이 들면 손톱 기름 성분까지 줄어 더 잘 마른다 → 설거지엔 고무장갑, 손 씻은 직후 손톱·큐티클 보습 → 손톱만 따로 바르기 쉬운 큐티클 오일.

## 주장별 원문 인용
1. 통념 반박 — 물에 자주 닿을수록 약해져 갈라짐 [대본 2문단]
   - AAD-TIPS: "Protect your nails from water. Too much water exposure can weaken your nails, causing them to split, peel, or break. To prevent this, wear gloves when cleaning or washing dishes, and make sure to moisturize after hand washing."
   - NHS(원인): "your hands often being in water or cleaning products"
2. 기전 — 약 25겹 세포층, 젖었다 마르기 반복 → 세포 사이 금 → 끝이 얇은 층으로 벗겨짐 [대본 3문단, 수치 25겹]
   - CHESSA: "the NP is a fully keratinized structure made up of about 25 layers of tightly packed keratinocytes, with a thickness of 0.5–1 mm"
   - CHESSA: "hydration and desiccation may play a significant role in household employees, hairdressers, and nurses where repeated wetting and drying of the hands leads to fractures between NP onychocytes."
   - CHESSA: "Lamellar onychoschizia: caused by the impairment of intercellular adhesive factors of the NP. As a result, the NP exfoliates into fine horizontal layers" / "typical of patients who wash their hands too frequently (e.g., homemakers, but also doctors and nurses)"
   - 블로그용(리무버·용제): "solvents … can dissolve intercellular lipids and damage intercellular cohesion" / "all methods of removing the applied preparations damage the healthy nail plates"
3. 나이·폐경 후 여성 — 기름 성분 감소 [대본 3문단 끝]
   - CHESSA: "normal nails contain 5% lipids and there is a decrease in cholesterol sulphate of the NP with age, especially in post-menopausal women, suggesting an important role of lipids in the development of NB"
   - CHESSA: "affecting up to 20% of the population, especially women over 50 years of age" / "In women, the intercellular keratinocyte bridges are constitutionally weaker than in males"
   - NHS: "It's normal for nails to: become thicker or break more easily (brittle) as you get older"
4. 고무장갑 [대본 4문단]
   - AAD-TIPS(1번 인용) · NHS: "wear rubber gloves if your hands are often in water or you regularly use cleaning products"
   - CHESSA(블로그용): "occlusive gloves applied over wet hands while working may be a cause of BN" / "wear cotton gloves under vinyl gloves for wet work"
5. 씻은 직후 손톱 보습 [대본 4문단, 기관명 미국피부과학회 1/2]
   - AAD-CARE: "Rub lotion on the fingernails. Nails need moisturizing to keep them flexible. The best time to apply lotion is immediately after washing the hands and after taking a bath or shower. This is especially important when the air is dry. Dry nails split more easily."
   - NHS: "apply hand cream to your nails and fingertips regularly"
6. 큐티클 오일 — 손톱 위·뿌리, 기름막 [대본 5문단]
   - CHESSA: "treatment includes … limiting contact with water and detergents, and regular use of emollients" / "Nail moisturizers are useful in patients with BN. They may contain occlusive material (petrolatum or lanolin) and humectants (glycerin, propylene glycol and proteins)."
   - "기름막이 물기를 덜 날아가게" = occlusive 보습제의 작용. 오일 자체의 임상시험 수치는 없음 → 수치·효능 비교 안 씀.
   - AAD-CARE(블로그용): "Cuticles protect the nail root, so it's best to not cut or push back the cuticles."
7. 6개월 [대본 6문단, 수치, 기관명 NHS 2/2]
   - NHS: "Fingernails that fall off after an injury should grow back within 6 months. Toenails can take up to 18 months."
