Carpal tunnel syndrome causes tingling, aching pain in your wrist and fingers. It happens when the tunnel around your median nerve gets squeezed — and these three habits are the biggest culprits.

First, holding your phone with your wrist bent for long stretches. Every extra hour of smartphone use in that bent position adds more strain to the nerve running through the tunnel.

Second, typing or using a mouse without wrist support. Bending your wrist up or sideways keeps steady pressure on that nerve all day.

Finally, sleeping with your wrist curled under your pillow. Blood flow slows at night, so that bent position squeezes the nerve even harder, which is why so many wake up with numb, tingling hands.

Here's the fix. Keep your wrist straight when you hold your phone, and stretch every 20 to 30 minutes. Add a wrist rest to your keyboard and mouse. And wear a wrist splint at night to keep your wrist from curling while you sleep.
