Doing everything right for stronger nails, but they still split at the tip, grow in thinner, or pick up ridges anyway? Three things you drink or eat most days could be working against you.

Nails are built almost entirely from keratin, a protein - and several nutrients help your body produce it. A few common food and drink habits get in the way.

Drinking wine or beer often can interfere with how your body absorbs and stores biotin - the nutrient your nail cells rely on most to build keratin.

Coffee's caffeine has a diuretic effect, and separately, compounds in coffee can reduce how much iron and zinc your gut absorbs from meals - both minerals nails need to grow strong.

Instant ramen and other packaged snacks are filling but nutrient-poor, so eating them often can leave you short on the protein and minerals nails are actually built from.

Try alternating alcohol with water and cutting back overall, and space coffee out from mealtimes so it doesn't compete with nutrient absorption. Swapping in more eggs or beans instead of instant noodles can help too.
