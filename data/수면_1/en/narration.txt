You tell yourself just one more video, and suddenly it's 1am. There's a name for that: revenge bedtime procrastination, and a recent national survey found most American adults do it regularly.

Scrolling delays melatonin with blue light, and diet makes it worse. Two out of three American adults don't get enough magnesium, so your nervous system stays wired even after the phone's down.

Three foods may help: kiwi, pistachios, and warm milk.

Research suggests two kiwis about an hour before bed can help you fall asleep faster and sleep longer.

Pistachios naturally contain melatonin, tryptophan, and magnesium.

Warm milk isn't just an old story either — casein peptides formed as milk digests have a calming effect on the nervous system.

These foods won't fix bedtime procrastination alone, but paired with a set phone cutoff, falling asleep gets a lot easier.
