Lying down at night and feeling that creeping, crawling itch deep in your legs that makes you want to move them, so you can't fall asleep? Restless legs syndrome is tied to dopamine signaling in the brain, and some everyday foods interfere with that signal or overstimulate your nerves.

Coffee, energy drinks, and even chocolate carry caffeine that overstimulates your nervous system and can block iron absorption, which your brain needs to make dopamine. Try herbal tea instead, or skip caffeine after 2pm.

Wine or beer might make you drowsy at first, but as it wears off later in the night, it can trigger a rebound in nerve activity that makes leg symptoms worse. Try to stop drinking at least 3 to 4 hours before bed.

Salty snacks like chips cause fluid retention that can disrupt sleep quality, and poor sleep tends to make restless legs feel worse. Reach for unsalted nuts or fresh fruit instead.

You don't have to cut all of these out completely - small changes in amount and timing can help. If you're low on iron, red meat and leafy greens like kale may help too.
