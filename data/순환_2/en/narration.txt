Rings tight by lunch, ankles thicker by evening - nothing about your day explains it.

Extra sodium traps water around your cells, and refined carbs make it worse by spiking insulin.

Deli meat, canned soup, and beer are three everyday culprits.

A few slices of deli ham can carry over a thousand milligrams of sodium.

Canned chicken noodle soup adds another 800 milligrams a cup.

Beer blocks the hormone that helps you hold fluid, so it overcorrects once alcohol fades - ankles look puffier the next morning.

Roast your own chicken or turkey at home instead of pre-sliced deli meat, check soup labels for under 500 milligrams a serving or simmer your own broth, and cap drinks at one or two with water alongside. Bananas and spinach help flush the sodium out.
