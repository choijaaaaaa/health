Been on your feet all day at work, and by the time you get home your ankles feel tight and the veins in your legs look more twisted than they did that morning?

Your leg veins have to push blood all the way back up to your heart, and certain everyday foods make that job harder by adding fluid volume or straining the vessel walls.

White bread and other low-fiber refined carbs make constipation more likely, and straining during a bowel movement spikes the pressure in your abdomen, which pushes back down on the veins in your legs.

Salty processed food like deli meats, frozen pizza, and fast food is loaded with sodium, and sodium makes your body hold onto extra water - that raises your blood volume and adds pressure on the veins in your legs.

Wine and other alcohol dilate your blood vessels while dehydrating you at the same time, which can weaken your veins' ability to push blood back up toward your heart.

Try trading white bread for whole grains or oats, swap deli meats and fast food for freshly cooked meat at home, and trade wine for sparkling water some nights. Staying well hydrated can help too.
