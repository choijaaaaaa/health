If you've started Wegovy, Zepbound, or Mounjaro and suddenly feel dizzy or lightheaded, it's not just the weight loss talking. Three real changes are behind it.

Dizziness shows up in up to one in ten people on these medications, most often in the first few weeks. Appetite suppression, fluid loss from nausea and diarrhea, and a sudden drop in blood pressure are usually why.

First, these drugs suppress appetite so strongly that daily food intake drops sharply. Less fuel means your blood sugar runs low, especially if you're also taking insulin or other diabetes medication. Eat small, protein-rich meals every few hours instead of skipping meals.

Second, nausea, vomiting, or diarrhea pull fluid and sodium out of your body fast. Don't just sip plain water. Add an electrolyte drink or salty broth to replace what you're losing.

Finally, rapid weight loss lowers blood pressure on its own. If you're already on blood pressure medication, your old dose may now be too strong, and standing up too fast can trigger a sudden head rush. Check your blood pressure regularly and ask your doctor whether your dosage still fits.

Track these three causes, and dizziness from your weight loss injection becomes something you can manage with confidence.
