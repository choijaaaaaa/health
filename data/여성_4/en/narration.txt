Face suddenly burning up mid-meeting, then drenched in sweat minutes later? Lower estrogen makes your body overreact to heat - coffee, wine, spicy food make it worse.

Coffee can worsen hot flashes and night sweats - a Mayo Clinic study of 1,800+ women found this link. Try decaf, or skip it after noon.

Wine and other alcohol widen blood vessels fast, causing a flush while you drink. Avoid it a few hours before bed, or pace it with water.

Spicy food like hot wings triggers the same heat-sensing nerves as a hot flash, so sweat and flushing often follow. Sipping ice water through the meal can help take the edge off.

None of this means giving these up for good - small tweaks in amount and timing help. More soy, like edamame or tofu, may help too - one review found it cut hot flash frequency over 20%.
