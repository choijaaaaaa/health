## 대본 문장별 근거

**1. 훅** "아기띠, 아기 다리가 아래로 쭉 늘어진 채 안고 다니시나요?"
- IHDI-RES: "narrow-base carriers [narrow support in groin area]" — 좁은 받침에선 받침이 사타구니에만 걸려 다리가 아래로 늘어진다(상황 묘사, 수치 주장 없음).

**2. 고관절·병 정의·시기** "고관절은 허벅지뼈 머리가 골반의 오목한 자리에 끼워진 관절이에요. 이 자리가 얕아 헐거운 게 고관절이형성증이고, 태어나 여섯 달 동안 빠르게 자리를 잡아요."
- NHS-DDH: "Developmental dysplasia of the hip (DDH) is where your baby's hip joint is loose and can move out of its normal position easily." · "The leg bone of babies with developmental dysplasia of the hip (DDH) does not fit properly into the space in their hip bone. This means the joint becomes looser and does not move properly."
- IHDI-BC: "Developmental dysplasia is caused by anomalies such as a shallow acetabulum, increased femoral anteversion, and capsular laxity." ("얕아" = shallow acetabulum)
- IHDI-RES: "This is especially important during the first six months of life when the hips are developing rapidly as they prepare for crawling and walking."

**3. 반전(아기띠는 원인 아님) + 자세 차이** "아기띠가 이 병을 만든다는 근거는 없어요. 다만 받침이 좁으면 다리가 일자로 늘어지고, 넓으면 무릎까지 받쳐져 M자로 벌어져요."
- IHDI-BC: "Based on the best available evidence, IHDI maintains that no baby carrier can cause or prevent hip dysplasia."
- IHDI-RES: "Although there is no evidence that narrow-base carriers cause hip dysplasia, the narrow-base carriers may not promote natural improvement." · "wide-base carriers [thighs supported to the knees] and in narrow-base carriers [narrow support in groin area]"
- IHDI-BW: "The M-position is a natural clinging position for infants – also known as the Spread-Squat, or Jockey Position."
- (블로그용) IHDI-SW: "Swaddling infants with the hips and knees in an extended position increases the risk of hip dysplasia and dislocation." · "The baby's legs should not be tightly wrapped straight down and pressed together." — 다리를 펴서 묶는 **속싸개**는 위험 요인으로 명시(아기띠와 구분).

**4. 연구 수치("실제로")** "실제로 아기띠에 탄 아기 열세 명을 초음파로 보니, 좁은 받침에선 뼈 머리가 자리에 덮인 정도가 10에서 12퍼센트 적었어요. 넓은 받침은 고관절 치료용 보조기와 차이가 없었고요."
- SIDDICKY: "We obtained coronal hip ultrasound images of 10 healthy full-term infants (13.5 ± 3.4 weeks, 5F/5M) and three full-term mild DDH infants (8.9 ± 4.0 weeks, 2F/1M) in three conditions: Pavlik harness, wide-base baby carrier, and narrow-base baby carrier." · "Femoral head coverage was 10%-12% lower for the narrow-base carrier." · "This research demonstrated that wide-base baby carriers held hips in a position that was not significantly different from the Pavlik harness, meaning that babywearing in a wide-base carrier may have benefits for healthy hip development."
- 보조기 = Pavlik harness. NHS-DDH: "The most common treatment involves your baby wearing a soft fabric harness (Pavlik harness) that helps gently guide their hip joint into the right position."
- ⚠️ 13명(건강 10 + 가벼운 DDH 3) 소규모, 착용 순간의 자세 측정이다. "넓은 받침이 병을 막는다/고친다" ✗ — 대본은 덮인 정도·보조기와 같은 자세까지만. 초록의 "may have benefits"도 단정으로 바꾸지 않았다.
- 화면 출처 줄: `출처: Siddicky 외, J Orthop Res 2023 (아기 13명 초음파) · 국제고관절이형성증연구소`

**5. 바꿔 쓸 제품(제안형)** "그래서 엉덩이 받침이 넓은 아기띠로 바꿔 두면 마음이 놓여요."
- IHDI-RES: "When choosing between a narrow-base carrier or a carrier that fully supports the thighs around the parent's torso, the wide-base carrier is more likely to promote healthy hip development." · "this research provides further support to the advice of the International Hip Dysplasia Institute for parents to use wide-base carriers when carrying infants prior to the age of six months."

**6. 고르는 기준** "고를 땐 받침이 양쪽 무릎 안쪽까지 오는지, 무릎이 엉덩이보다 조금 높은지 보세요."
- 받침 폭: IHDI-RES "wide-base carriers [thighs supported to the knees]"
- 무릎 높이: IHDI-BW "This is recommended as a healthy habit with the thighs spread around the mother's torso and the hips bent so the knees are slightly higher than the buttocks with the thighs supported."
- 대본 미사용(블로그용): IHDI-BW "the IHDI recommends inward-facing during this period of rapid hip development" — 첫 여섯 달은 바깥 보기보다 **안쪽(엄마) 보기**. "Less spread with more flexion is healthy, as is slightly more spread with less flexion as the child grows." — 신생아는 덜 벌리고 더 굽히게(받침 폭 조절 기능이 있으면 좋다는 근거).

**7. 병원 신호** "고관절에서 딸깍 소리가 나거나, 두 다리의 길이나 움직임이 다르면 소아과에 가세요."
- NHS-DDH: "a clicking or popping sound when they move their hips" · "one leg moves differently to the other during things like play and nappy changes" · "their legs are different lengths" · "Non-urgent advice: See a GP if: you think you or your child may have developmental dysplasia of the hip"
- 대본 미사용(블로그용): NHS-DDH 위험 요인 "they're a girl" · "someone else in your family has had it" · "they moved into a breech position" · 진단 "DDH is diagnosed in babies using an ultrasound scan of their hip." · "Developmental dysplasia of the hip (DDH) is not usually painful, so many parents do not realise their baby has it."
