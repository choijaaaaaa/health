Waking up with a sore jaw on nights you weren't even stressed? The real cause might not be stress at all.

Three independent triggers behind sleep bruxism are acid reflux, genetics, and ADHD medication.

Acid reflux is one of the strongest risk factors for grinding - people with GERD are up to six times more likely to grind at night, since acid creeping up the esophagus can trigger brief awakenings that set the jaw clenching.

Genetics plays a bigger role than most people realize. Twin studies show genetics explains roughly half the risk, and kids are about 50% more likely to grind if a parent had a history of it too.

Stimulant medication for ADHD, like Adderall or Ritalin, is another overlooked cause - it raises dopamine activity in a way that also ramps up jaw muscle activity, often within weeks of starting.

Frequent heartburn? Treating the reflux can ease the grinding too. Family history of it? Mention it at your next dental checkup so any wear gets caught early. And if grinding started after a new stimulant prescription, talk to your doctor about the dose - it's a documented side effect.
