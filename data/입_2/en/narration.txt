Feel that same sting hit the same sore spot again? A few foods could be why.

Zinc deficiency shows up in 28% of people with recurring sores, versus just 4% of everyone else.

Citrus, chocolate, and coffee are the three biggest culprits. Citrus fruits are highly acidic and sting right on an open sore - swap them for a milder fruit like banana while it heals.

Chocolate can trigger an allergic-type reaction in sensitive mouths. When you want something sweet, reach for honey instead - studies even show it can help the sore heal faster.

Coffee's acidic too, and heavy intake is linked to more flare-ups - swap it for herbal tea instead.

Most sores heal within a week or two - if one lingers longer, get it checked.
