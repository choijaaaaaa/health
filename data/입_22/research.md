## 주장별 원문 인용
1. 훅·모르고 간다 — ADA: "Most people aren't aware that they are clenching or grinding their teeth. Dentists and dental hygienists are often the first to notice the signs"
2. 의지로 못 멈춤 — CC: "You can't always stop yourself from teeth grinding or clenching, especially if you brux during sleep." / "Grinding or clenching your teeth in your sleep can cause more harm than bruxing during waking hours because you don't realize it's happening."
3. 잠들면 훨씬 세게 — MSD: "The most severe and extensive grinding and clenching occurs during sleep." / SF: "A sleeping person cannot perceive their bite strength, so they may clench more tightly"
   - ⚠️ SF의 "up to 250 pounds of force"는 머크 매뉴얼 재인용이라 원문 미확인 → 안 씀. 깨어 있을 때 대비 몇 배인지 수치도 안 씀.
4. 닳고 금 가고 턱 아픔 — NHS: "a painful jaw … worn-down or broken teeth, which can cause increased sensitivity, and loss of teeth and fillings / headaches / earache / disturbed sleep"; ADA: "chipped or cracked teeth or worn, damaged spots"; MSD: "abrade and eventually wear down enamel and dentin … damage metal or ceramic dental crowns, and cause teeth to become mobile"
5. 스트레스·잠 문제 — NHS: "often linked to: stress, anxiety … sleep problems like snoring, sleep apnoea, taking medicines for sleep problems, depression or anxiety, smoking, drinking lots of alcohol or caffeine"; ADA: "Teeth that don't line up properly / Missing or crooked teeth"
6. 마우스피스 = 이끼리 안 닿게, 멈추는 게 아님 — MSD: "Plastic oral appliances (night guards) that prevent occlusal contact by fitting between the teeth can be used while sleeping." / NHS: "A dentist may recommend a mouth guard or mouth splint. These are worn at night and protect your teeth from damage." / SF: "There is no treatment that can completely eliminate or cure teeth grinding during sleep" / NIDCR: "fit over the teeth and protect the teeth … They do not change the teeth or bite … Make sure it is not designed to permanently change your bite. Stop using it and consult your dentist or doctor if it causes pain."
7. 관리 — ADA-MG: "Rinse before and after each use or brush with a toothbrush and toothpaste. Regularly clean the mouthguard in cool, soapy water … Store and transport the mouthguard in a sturdy container that has vents so it can dry … Never leave the mouthguard in the sun or in hot water. … bring your mouthguard for an evaluation … replace it immediately if it shows sign of wear, is damaged or ill fitting." (스포츠 마우스가드 페이지 — 블로그엔 '마우스가드 관리 안내'로 표기)
8. 수치 — 얼음 20분: NHS "use an ice pack (or bag of frozen peas) wrapped in a tea towel for 20 to 30 minutes to help reduce jaw pain or swelling". 원고는 물결표 없이 "20분쯤", 블로그는 "20분에서 30분".
9. 고르는 기준 — ADA: "A custom guard … will give you the closest, most comfortable fit. Night guards are also sold online and in stores, but may not provide the same protection as a guard made just for you." / "night guards are not the same as sports guards … extra cushioning that probably won't feel comfortable for sleep."; ADA-MG boil and bite: "first softened in water (boiled), then inserted and allowed to adapt to the shape of your mouth. Always follow the manufacturers' instructions."; MSD: "if the only problem is tooth wear, over-the-counter (OTC) heat-moldable devices fitted at home are available, but a dental evaluation should first be done to assess the severity of wear and determine whether an OTC device is adequate."
10. 병원 신호 — NIDCR: "clicking or popping sounds in the TMJs, without pain, are common, are considered normal" / "Limited movement or locking of the jaw / Painful clicking, popping, or grating … / A change in the way the upper and lower teeth fit together"; NHS: "See a dentist if: you grind your teeth and have tooth damage or sensitive teeth / pain in your jaw, face or ear / your partner says you're grinding"; CC: "Research has confirmed a correlation between sleep apnea and teeth grinding … unclear whether sleep apnea causes bruxism"; ADA: "sleep study can measure what's happening while you sleep"; NHS: "can be made by a dentist to fit precisely".
