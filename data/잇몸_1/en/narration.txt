Brushing and flossing perfectly, but your gums still bleed? The cause might not be in your bathroom cabinet at all.

Bleeding gums usually mean your immune system is fighting gumline inflammation - and three habits quietly fuel that: chronic stress, vaping, and not drinking enough water.

First, chronic stress. It raises cortisol, weakening immune response and feeding inflammation - higher stress is linked to more plaque and more bleeding when you brush.

Second, vaping. The aerosol disrupts bacteria in your mouth and ramps up inflammation at the gumline - research links vaping to more plaque and more bleeding on probing.

Third, running low on water. Trading water for coffee and soda all day dries out your mouth, and less saliva means less protection for your gums.

Try a short walk or deep breathing to ease stress, free quitline support like 1-800-QUIT-NOW can help if you vape, and keep water nearby to sip instead of coffee or soda.
