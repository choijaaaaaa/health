Started a protein shake, an iron pill, or a daily antacid recently - and now you're straining on the toilet for the first time in years?

Fiber pulls water into your stool and keeps it moving - without enough, stool turns small and dry, and each of these works against that.

A scoop of whey protein has almost no fiber, and digesting that much protein makes your kidneys use extra water to flush out the waste - so there's less left for your stool.

Iron pills are common for anemia or pregnancy, but your gut only absorbs part of each dose - the rest irritates your intestines and slows digestion.

Calcium in antacids like Tums neutralizes stomach acid to calm heartburn, but that same calcium also tightens your gut's smooth muscle, slowing stool moving along.

Add chia seeds and extra water to your shake, take iron with vitamin C, and ask your pharmacist about a magnesium antacid instead.
