Most men who reach their 50s and 60s already get up at least once a night to pee - most just blame getting older.

An enlarged prostate presses on the bladder, so it signals sooner than it should. Some foods make that worse.

Caffeine blocks a hormone that helps your kidneys hold onto water, so you make more urine. Switch to decaf after 3pm.

Alcohol does the same thing, plus it irritates the bladder and can swell the prostate. Finish drinking 3 to 4 hours before bed.

A salty dinner makes you thirstier later, so you drink more right before bed. Go lighter on sodium at night instead.

Watching the timing and amount in the evening can cut down how often you wake up.
