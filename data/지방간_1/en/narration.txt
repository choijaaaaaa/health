Tired no matter how much you rest, with a dull ache under your right ribs? Your liver usually stays silent, but these can be early signs.

Fast food is a big cause. Getting 20% or more of your daily calories from burgers and fries is linked to significantly higher liver fat, a 2023 USC study found.

Soda is another one, and diet soda isn't the safe swap you'd think. A 2025 UK Biobank study of 120,000 adults found sugary drinks raised fatty liver risk by 50%, diet soda by 60%.

Red meat matters too. People who eat the most have about 27% higher odds of fatty liver, since saturated fat adds strain on the liver.

Swap fast food for grilled chicken, soda for sparkling water, and red meat for fish a few times a week — small swaps that take real pressure off your liver.
