Most women will deal with a vaginal yeast infection at some point - and something in your everyday diet might be part of why it keeps coming back.

Candida, one of the fungi behind vaginal yeast infections, thrives on sugar - so a diet heavy in sugary foods can give it more fuel to grow.

Cookies, cake, and candy send your blood sugar up fast, and that spike can create conditions where candida grows more easily.

Refined carbs like white bread and white rice work the same way - they break down into sugar quickly and raise blood sugar just as fast.

Beer and sweet wine combine sugar and alcohol, which can swing your blood sugar and put extra strain on your immune system at the same time.

Try swapping sweets for nuts or a boiled egg, white bread for whole grain, and beer or sweet wine for sparkling water. Unsweetened yogurt with live cultures may help too.
