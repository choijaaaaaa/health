Nasal polyps, if congestion and smell loss keep coming back, this might not be simple allergies.

Nasal polyps are soft growths from long-term inflammation in your nasal and sinus lining. They're more common in people with asthma, allergies, or a sensitivity to certain pain relievers. A few habits can make them worse.

First, taking aspirin, ibuprofen, or naproxen without knowing your sensitivity. Some people have a reaction called AERD, where these drugs suddenly worsen congestion. Talk to your doctor about acetaminophen instead, and mention any drug reactions.

Second, breathing in tobacco smoke, chemical fumes, or dust without protection keeps the swelling and inflammation going. Try an air purifier indoors and a mask around fumes or dust.

Last, skipping saline rinse and letting mucus sit lets irritation keep polyps growing. Use a saline rinse once or twice a day, and run a humidifier at home.

Watching pain reliever choices, limiting irritants, and saline rinsing may genuinely help. If congestion or smell loss lasts more than 2 weeks, an ENT visit is worth considering.
