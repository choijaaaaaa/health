If your partner keeps elbowing you awake over your snoring, before you blame your age, check what you ate before bed.

Snoring happens when your airway narrows during sleep and the soft tissue around it vibrates as air squeezes through. Three foods common on the American dinner table can each narrow that airway differently.

First, processed meats like bacon and deli cold cuts. They're loaded with sodium and nitrates, which may contribute to fluid retention and swelling in throat tissue.

Second, soda and other sugary drinks. Drinking them regularly can drive weight gain over time, and extra fat around the neck thickens the airway wall from the outside.

Third, spicy, greasy takeout like hot wings. That combo can trigger acid reflux, and stomach acid creeping into the throat may irritate airway tissue right as you lie down.

Try freshly cooked chicken or turkey breast instead of bacon and deli meat, sparkling water instead of soda, and finish spicy or fried meals at least three hours before bedtime. Sleeping on your side instead of your back helps too.
