Thick yellow-green mucus and a nose that won't clear? That's sinusitis - swollen, blocked sinus openings trap fluid inside and fuel the inflammation. Three everyday habits make that blockage worse.

First, blowing your nose too hard. A hard blow creates about ten times more pressure than a cough, and that pressure pushes mucus straight into your sinus cavities.

Second, using decongestant spray for more than three days. It shrinks blood vessels fast, but push past that limit and your nasal lining rebounds, swelling worse than before - doctors call it rhinitis medicamentosa.

Finally, spending hours in dry indoor air. Low humidity thickens mucus and slows the cilia that sweep it out, so drainage backs up.

Blow one nostril at a time, gently. Cap decongestant spray at three days. Keep indoor humidity near 40 to 50 percent, and drink plenty of water to thin the mucus. If yellow-green congestion drags on past ten days, see a doctor.
