Woke up with your big toe swollen, red, and too painful for a sock? That's gout - sharp uric acid crystals in the joint. It affects 9.2 million Americans, and three seafood favorites could be why.

Shrimp cocktail carries about 120 milligrams of purine per 100 grams, and a U.S. study found each extra weekly seafood serving raises gout risk by 7%.

Sardines go further - up to 480 milligrams per 100 grams, why Mayo Clinic lists them among the seafood to limit.

Anchovies pack even more - around 500 milligrams per 100 grams, among the highest purine content of any restaurant food.

Swap shrimp cocktail for deviled eggs instead - eggs are a genuinely low-purine protein. Trade sardines for tofu, and swap the anchovy dressing for a plain oil-and-vinegar vinaigrette.
