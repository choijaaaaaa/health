Three food coma myths keep tricking you every day. Save this before your next lunch.

Myth: it's the tryptophan in turkey putting you to sleep. Fact: researchers say tryptophan alone rarely explains real drowsiness. It's the carb-heavy, oversized meal built around it, spiking your blood sugar, then crashing it, that actually does the damage.

Myth: you're just tired from your morning. Fact: a high-fat meal triggers your immune system to release cytokines, small proteins tied to fatigue. Fried food and heavy sauces set this off, on top of whatever your blood sugar is doing.

Myth: you just need more willpower to push through. Fact: your body clock dips between two and four in the afternoon, the same low point your brain hits overnight, just twelve hours later. Lunch just lands right in the middle of it.

Lead your plate with protein and fiber, keep the fried and heavy stuff to a smaller portion, and eat until satisfied, not stuffed. For the two o'clock dip specifically, a short nap before two, or a few minutes in bright light, helps reset your alertness.
