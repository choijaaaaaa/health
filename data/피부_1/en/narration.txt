Losing collagen faster than you should? Before you reach for another serum, stop and check three habits that break collagen down faster than any cream can fix.

First, smoking. Cigarette smoke triggers enzymes called MMPs that break down collagen and elastic fibers, and smokers develop two to three times more facial wrinkles than non-smokers the same age.

Second, unprotected sun exposure. UV rays trigger that same collagen-degrading MMP response, and studies show even sun exposure too mild to cause redness is enough to keep that response switched on.

Third, a high-sugar diet. Excess sugar binds to collagen through a process called glycation, forming stiff structures that replace healthy, flexible collagen.

Quitting smoking lets collagen levels bounce back — most studies show real improvement within 4 to 8 weeks. Wearing sunscreen daily cuts wrinkle and pigmentation formation by 30 to 50 percent in clinical trials. And cutting back on added sugar slows glycation before it locks in.
