Ever notice your lower back feels stiffer after a long commute than after an actual workout?

Sitting compresses spinal discs and slows blood flow - certain foods make it worse.

Salty snacks, alcohol, and sugary treats are three common culprits.

A bag of chips or fast food can pack in over half a day's sodium, and extra sodium may cause fluid retention. Air-popped popcorn satisfies the craving with far less salt.

Several drinks after work can dehydrate you and hurt your sleep, cutting into muscle recovery. Water plus a banana before bed helps offset that.

Sugary soda and pastries cause quick blood sugar swings that may fuel inflammation. Sparkling water or fruit works instead.

Cutting back on sodium, alcohol, and sugar is a simple place to start.
