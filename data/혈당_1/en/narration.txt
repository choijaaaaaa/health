White bread, soda, and other refined carbs can spike your blood sugar after a meal, then crash it soon after - repeated spikes like that are linked to growing insulin resistance.

I'm apple cider vinegar. Arizona State University researchers found my acetic acid can blunt that post-meal surge - people with insulin resistance needed less insulin for a high-carb meal after taking me first.

A follow-up study found a spoonful at bedtime kept waking blood sugar steadier the next morning in adults with type 2 diabetes.

Just dilute me in water - taken straight, I can wear down enamel and irritate your throat. On diabetes medication? Check with your doctor first.

I'm not a substitute for treatment. Exercise and a balanced plate still matter most.
