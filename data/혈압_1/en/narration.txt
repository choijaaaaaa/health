Two sugary drinks a day alone raised blood pressure risk 52 percent over 25 years - and soda's not the only culprit. Deli meat and alcohol play a role too.

Six slices of deli turkey or ham pack half your daily sodium limit. Ask for freshly roasted chicken breast instead, or pick "low-sodium" if it's packaged.

Heavy drinking stiffens blood vessel walls. Moderating it dropped systolic pressure by 5.5 points in Mayo Clinic research - try hibiscus tea instead, shown in a Tufts study to cut it by about 7 points in six weeks.

And soda's sugar spikes insulin, making your body hold extra sodium. Swap it for sparkling water instead - same fizz, none of the sugar.
